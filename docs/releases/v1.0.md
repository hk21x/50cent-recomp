The first release of the unofficial native PC port of 50 Cent: Bulletproof (original Xbox). It is not an emulator:
the game's own code is statically recompiled into a Windows program, with a new renderer, sound and input underneath.

**No game files are included.** You need your own copy of the game: the USA/Europe Xbox release, as an `.iso` of your
disc or a folder with its files. The game is built on your PC from your disc.

## What's in it

- **True widescreen**, 21:9 and wider included, with the HUD at the screen's edges; the aspect changes as you play.
- **60 frames a second** (the console ran at 30), with the game playing at its own speed.
- **Sharper graphics:** render scale up to 200%, MSAA up to 8x, FXAA or SMAA, sharpening, anisotropic filtering,
  enhanced world textures, and sharp text and HUD.
- **Mouse and keyboard:** WASD and direct mouse aim, the button prompts showing your keys, and a mouse pointer in the
  menus, the shops and the name keyboard.
- **Controllers:** Xbox, PlayStation and Switch pads with hot plugging and rumble, and modern button pictures.
- **PC Settings in the game:** Y on the Options page opens them in the PDA; F1 opens the settings window anywhere.
- **22 achievements**, a field of view setting, a levelled sound mix, skip the intro movies, hide the HUD (F9), F12
  screenshots and Quit Game on the title menu.

## Getting started

1. Download **BulletproofLauncher.exe** below and put it in a folder of its own with a short path (for example
   `C:\Games\Bulletproof`).
2. Run it. Windows may warn about an unrecognised app: **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Choose your disc image (or folder) and press **Get the game ready**. The launcher downloads the game's package
   (about 75 MB: the program and a compiler, no game code) into its own folder, and your PC builds the game's code
   from your disc in about a minute. Nothing is installed in Windows. You can stop at any time; it carries on next
   time.
4. Press **Play**. In the game, **F1** opens the PC settings.

Requirements: Windows 10 or 11 (64 bit), a Direct3D 12 graphics card (Direct3D 11 also works) and about 400 MB
free besides your copy of the game.

## Known issues

- Tested on AMD and Intel Arc graphics; NVIDIA cards are expected to work but have not been tested yet.
- Many of the story's missions have been played on PC, but not yet the whole story start to finish, nor the arcade.

Problems and questions: the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/PQjkKsdtc). Please include `launcher.log` (beside the
launcher) or the log in `Bulletproof\play\logs`.
